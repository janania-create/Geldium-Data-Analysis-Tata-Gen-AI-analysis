Geldium AI-Powered Collections Strategy Project
Project Overview

This project simulates the role of an AI and analytics consultant working with Geldium and Tata iQ to improve delinquency prediction and collections strategy using data analytics, predictive modeling, and responsible AI principles. The objective was to analyze customer financial behavior, identify delinquency risk factors, design predictive interventions, and propose an AI-powered autonomous collections system that balances operational efficiency with fairness and transparency.

The project was completed across four structured tasks, progressing from exploratory data analysis to executive-level AI strategy design.

Task 1 — Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
Objective

The goal of Task 1 was to perform an Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on Geldium’s customer delinquency dataset to understand data quality, identify anomalies, uncover missing values, and detect early indicators of delinquency risk before predictive modeling.

Key Activities Performed
Dataset Understanding
Reviewed customer financial and behavioral variables using the dataset description guide.
Identified numerical, categorical, and binary variables relevant to delinquency prediction.
Analyzed customer payment behavior patterns across multiple months.
Data Quality Assessment
Detected missing values in critical variables such as:
Income
Loan Balance
Credit Score
Investigated inconsistencies and outliers, including unusually high credit utilization rates.
Checked for duplicate customer records and structural anomalies.
Missing Data Treatment Strategy
Applied median imputation for skewed financial variables.
Applied mean imputation for low-frequency missing numerical values.
Evaluated whether synthetic data generation was necessary.
Risk Indicator Identification

The EDA identified several high-risk indicators associated with delinquency:

Repeated late or missed payments
High credit utilization
Elevated debt-to-income ratios
Lower credit scores
Short account tenure
